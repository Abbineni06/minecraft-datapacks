🍏 Step-by-Step Installation Guide (macOS & TLauncher)

Follow these quick steps to install any datapack in seconds without unzipping.

1. Locate your Minecraft Saves Folder on macOS:

In Finder, press Cmd + Shift + G and paste:

~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves/

2. Copy the Downloaded .ZIP File:

Open your target world folder, open the datapacks/ directory inside, and drag & drop the downloaded .zip file directly inside. Do NOT unzip it!

3. Activate In-Game:

Join your world, press T to open chat, and type:

/reload

Verify with /datapack list enabled. The datapack will appear highlighted in green!
